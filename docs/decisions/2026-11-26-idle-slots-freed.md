# 2026-11-26: a session idle at its prompt in review or merged frees its worker slot

The audit of 2026-11-26 counted 260 refusals "all 8 worker slots are taken" while sessions of `in_review` and `merged` tasks sat idle at their prompts: the worst waits were 39 h in `merged`, 21.5 h in review and 20 h in `closing`. `TaskState::takes_slot` counts all three, and the stuck check covered `working` only, so nothing noticed them.

A session of an `in_review` or `merged` task that runs nothing (no job, part, background work or shell) and has made no hook call, run no tool and changed no screen for `[watch] idle_free_review_min` (60) or `idle_free_merged_min` (30) now counts as waiting, exactly like a session in an `AskUserQuestion` dialog: the tick sets `sessions.idle_since` (migration 0025), raises the standing finding `idle-slot` with a notice, and the `queue` stage that follows may start a queued task, still bounded by `max_workers` plus `idle_overflow`. The session's next hook call clears the mark and takes the slot back at once. The stuck threshold of those two states is the idle-free time, so one finding fires, not two; a limit of 0 turns the freeing off and leaves the ordinary `stuck_after_min` finding. The stuck check also covers `closing` with a live session, which is only reported: a closing session whose session ended already frees its slot, and one whose session lives is closing on its own account.

Closing idle sessions automatically was rejected: closing stays on the usual confirm path (`ask_before_close`, the merged grace), so a session that may still have something to say is never ended by the tick. `headwork doctor` check `idle-slots` warns when either limit is 0.
